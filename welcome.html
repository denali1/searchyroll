Welcome to Searchyroll

Searchyroll adds a unified, advanced search layer across Crunchyroll and Hidive so you can find anime by genre, dub/sub availability, completion status, and studio — across both platforms at once.

How to use it

Press Ctrl+Shift+Y (or Cmd+Shift+Y on Mac) on any Crunchyroll or Hidive page to open the search overlay. You can also click the floating Searchyroll button in the bottom-right corner of the page.

About your catalog

Your search catalog builds automatically as you browse Crunchyroll and Hidive. The more you browse, the more titles become searchable. Searches run entirely against your local catalog — no network calls at search time.

Searchyroll is open source. Your browsing data stays local to your browser.
